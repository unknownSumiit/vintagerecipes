Supper Classics Recipes

These are the dinners that came out of the oven on weeknights and Sundays for generations: hearty, thrifty, and made from scratch. Many of them stretched a pound of ground beef to feed a whole family.

Each recipe is written with modern measurements and safe cooking temperatures, but the flavor is just the way you remember it.
