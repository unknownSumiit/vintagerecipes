Homemade Cinnamon Rolls

By the Vintage Recipe Box team · Updated November 16, 2026

Soft, fluffy homemade cinnamon rolls with a buttery brown sugar and cinnamon swirl and a cream cheese icing.

Bake sale classic
📜 A little history · Bake sale classic

Cinnamon rolls trace their roots to the Swedish kanelbulle and similar Northern European sweet breads. In America, they became a bake sale, church breakfast, and holiday morning tradition, baked from scratch in a 9x13 pan and passed down on well-worn recipe cards.

Prep30 min
Cook25 min

Makes12 rolls

Few things fill a house with warmth like a pan of homemade cinnamon rolls rising in the kitchen. These are soft and pillowy, swirled with buttery brown sugar and cinnamon, and finished with a cream cheese icing.

The dough is simple and forgiving. You do not need a mixer, just a big bowl, a wooden spoon, and a little patience while the dough rises.

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, stir the yeast and sugar into the warm milk. Let stand until foamy, about 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in the melted butter, eggs, and salt. Add the flour one cup at a time, stirring until a soft dough forms.
  3. Turn the dough onto a floured counter and knead for 5 to 7 minutes, until smooth and elastic, adding a little flour if it is very sticky.
  4. Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot until doubled, about 1 hour.
  5. Roll the dough into a 16x12-inch rectangle. Spread with the soft butter, then sprinkle evenly with the brown sugar and cinnamon.
  6. Starting from a long side, roll the dough up tightly. Cut into 12 even slices with a sharp knife or unflavored dental floss.
  7. Arrange the rolls in a greased 9x13-inch pan. Cover and let rise until puffy, 30 to 45 minutes. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 350°F.
  8. Bake for 22 to 25 minutes, until golden brown.
  9. Beat the cream cheese, butter, powdered sugar, and vanilla until smooth, adding milk as needed. Spread over the warm rolls.

Tips for success

  • Milk that is too hot can kill the yeast. It should feel comfortably warm on your wrist, like a baby’s bottle.
  • Make ahead: after cutting the rolls and placing them in the pan, cover and refrigerate overnight. In the morning, let them rise at room temperature for about an hour, then bake.

Storing and reheating

Keep covered at room temperature for 1 day or refrigerate for up to 4 days. Warm individual rolls in the microwave for 15 to 20 seconds. Freeze un-iced rolls for up to 2 months.
