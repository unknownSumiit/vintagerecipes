Old-Fashioned Baked Macaroni and Cheese

By the Vintage Recipe Box team · Updated November 16, 2026

Creamy, cheesy baked macaroni and cheese made from scratch with a sharp cheddar sauce and a buttery, golden crumb topping.

1800s classic
📜 A little history · 1800s classic

Baked macaroni and cheese has been on American tables far longer than the blue box. Thomas Jefferson served macaroni dishes at the White House, and a recipe for baked macaroni with cheese appeared in Mary Randolph’s The Virginia House-Wife in 1824. By the mid-1900s, a golden casserole of baked macaroni was a Sunday dinner and holiday staple.

This is the real thing: elbow macaroni folded into a creamy homemade cheddar sauce, then baked until bubbly with a crisp, buttery crumb topping. It is the kind of macaroni and cheese that gets scraped from the corners of the dish.

Sharp cheddar gives the best flavor, and a little dry mustard, an old-fashioned secret, makes the cheese taste even cheesier without tasting of mustard.

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. Cook the macaroni in salted water for 2 minutes less than the package directions. Drain.
  3. In a large saucepan, melt the butter over medium heat. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Gradually whisk in the warm milk. Cook, stirring often, until the sauce is smooth and thick enough to coat a spoon, 5 to 7 minutes.
  5. Remove from the heat. Stir in the dry mustard, salt, pepper, and 3 cups of the cheese until melted.
  6. Stir the macaroni into the sauce and pour into the baking dish. Sprinkle with the remaining 1 cup of cheese.
  7. Toss the breadcrumbs with the melted butter and scatter over the top. Dust with paprika.
  8. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until bubbly and golden. Let stand 5 minutes before serving.

Tips for success

  • Shred your own cheese from a block. Pre-shredded cheese contains anti-caking powder that can make the sauce grainy.
  • Warming the milk first helps the sauce come together smoothly and quickly.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Reheat individual portions in the microwave with a splash of milk, stirring halfway.
