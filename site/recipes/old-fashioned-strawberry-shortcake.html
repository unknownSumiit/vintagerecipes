Old-Fashioned Strawberry Shortcake

By the Vintage Recipe Box team · Updated November 16, 2026

Old-fashioned strawberry shortcake with tender, flaky cream biscuits, sweet juicy strawberries, and fresh whipped cream.

1850s
📜 A little history · 1850s

Strawberry shortcake has been an American summer tradition since the mid-1800s, when towns held “strawberry shortcake socials” to celebrate the berry harvest. The original shortcake is not a sponge cake but a tender, lightly sweet biscuit, split and piled with juicy berries and cream.

Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Total1 hr 5 min
Serves6
Jump to Recipe

Real old-fashioned strawberry shortcake starts with a warm, tender biscuit, not a spongy cake. Split open and piled with sugared strawberries and billowy whipped cream, it is the dessert that announced summer for generations.

The strawberries sit with a little sugar while you make the biscuits, so they release their sweet, ruby-red juices.

Instructions

  1. Toss the strawberries with the sugar and let them stand for 30 minutes, stirring once or twice.
  2. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Cut in the cold butter with a pastry blender or your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs with some pea-size pieces.
  4. Stir in the cream just until a shaggy dough forms. Turn it out and pat gently into a round about 3/4 inch thick.
  5. Cut out 6 biscuits with a 3-inch cutter, gently re-patting the scraps. Place on the baking sheet, brush the tops with cream, and sprinkle with sugar.
  6. Bake for 14 to 16 minutes, until golden. Let cool for 10 minutes.
  7. Whip the cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla until soft peaks form.
  8. Split each biscuit in half. Spoon strawberries and their juice over the bottom half, add whipped cream, and cover with the top. Finish with more berries and cream.

Tips for success

  • Keep the butter and cream very cold. Cold butter makes the flakiest biscuits.
  • Press the cutter straight down without twisting so the biscuits rise tall and even.

Storing and reheating

Shortcake is best assembled just before serving. Store biscuits in an airtight container for 1 day, and keep the berries and whipped cream refrigerated separately.
