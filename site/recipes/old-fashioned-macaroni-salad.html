Old-Fashioned Macaroni Salad

By the Vintage Recipe Box team · Updated November 16, 2026

Creamy, tangy, old-fashioned macaroni salad with celery, onion, and sweet relish, just like the one at every summer picnic.

1950s picnic
📜 A little history · 1950s picnic

Creamy macaroni salad rose to fame alongside the American backyard cookout in the years after World War II, when jarred mayonnaise and inexpensive dried pasta were in every pantry. It became the faithful companion to hot dogs, fried chicken, and barbecue at picnics and family reunions across the country.

Prep15 min
Cook10 min

This is the macaroni salad of family reunions and Fourth of July picnics: tender elbow macaroni in a creamy, slightly sweet and tangy dressing, with a little crunch from celery and onion.

Make it a few hours ahead so the flavors can blend. It is even better the next day.

8 servings

  • For the dressing
  • For the salad

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni in salted water according to the package directions until tender. Drain, rinse under cold water, and drain well.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together all of the dressing ingredients.
  3. Add the macaroni, celery, onion, bell pepper, relish, and eggs. Stir until evenly coated.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Stir before serving, adding a spoonful more mayonnaise or a splash of milk if the salad seems dry. Sprinkle with paprika.

Tips for success

  • Pasta keeps soaking up dressing as it chills. Reserve a few tablespoons of dressing to stir in just before serving.
  • Keep macaroni salad cold. At a picnic, nest the bowl in a larger bowl of ice.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 4 days. Do not freeze.
