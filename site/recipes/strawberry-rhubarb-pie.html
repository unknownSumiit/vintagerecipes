Strawberry Rhubarb Pie

By the Vintage Recipe Box team · Updated November 16, 2026

Sweet-tart strawberry rhubarb pie with a flaky double crust, the farmhouse favorite of early summer.

Farmhouse classic
📜 A little history · Farmhouse classic

Rhubarb was so commonly baked into pies on American farms that it earned the nickname “pie plant.” Because it is one of the first crops of spring, a rhubarb pie was a welcome treat after a long winter, and pairing it with the season’s first strawberries became a farmhouse tradition.

Prep30 min
Cook1 hr

Strawberry rhubarb pie is the taste of early summer: sweet strawberries and tart rhubarb baked together into a jammy, ruby-red filling under a flaky golden crust.

If you have never baked with rhubarb, do not worry. It cooks down into a soft, tangy filling, and the strawberries balance its tartness perfectly.

Instructions

  1. Place a rack in the lower third of the oven and heat to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with foil.
  2. In a large bowl, toss the rhubarb, strawberries, sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, and salt. Let stand for 10 minutes.
  3. Fit one crust into a 9-inch pie plate. Pour in the filling and dot with the butter.
  4. Top with the second crust (or a lattice). Trim, fold the edges under, and crimp. Cut several slits in the top crust.
  5. Brush with the egg wash and sprinkle with sugar. Set the pie on the foil-lined baking sheet.
  6. Bake for 20 minutes. Reduce the oven to 375°F and bake 35 to 45 minutes more, until the crust is deep golden and the filling bubbles thickly through the slits. Cover the edges with foil if they brown too quickly.
  7. Cool for at least 3 hours so the filling can set before slicing.

Tips for success

  • Only eat rhubarb stalks. The leaves are not edible, so trim them off and discard them.
  • Frozen rhubarb and strawberries work too. Do not thaw them, and add 10 to 15 minutes to the baking time.

Storing and reheating

Keep covered at room temperature for 1 day or refrigerate for up to 4 days.
