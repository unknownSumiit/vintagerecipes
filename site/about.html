About Us

The Vintage Recipe Box keeps the recipes from Grandma’s kitchen alive, written so anyone can make them today.

Our story

So many family recipes live on faded index cards, in the margins of church cookbooks, or only in someone’s memory. When those cards are lost, a little piece of family history goes with them. We started The Vintage Recipe Box to gather the classic American dishes of the 1800s through the 1960s, the suppers, potluck favorites, pies, and cakes that generations grew up with, and to make them easy to cook in a modern kitchen.

What makes our recipes different

  • The story behind every dish. Each recipe includes a short history of where it came from and why it became a favorite.
  • Modern measurements. No more guessing at “a moderate oven” or “butter the size of an egg.” Every recipe uses exact temperatures and standard measurements.
  • Everyday ingredients. Our recipes use ingredients found in any American grocery store.
  • Cooking for two or a crowd. Every recipe can be halved or doubled with a single tap.
  • Food safety first. We include safe internal temperatures and follow USDA guidance in our storage advice, even where old recipes did not.

Designed for easy reading

We built The Vintage Recipe Box to be comfortable to use on a phone or tablet in the kitchen. The text is large and high-contrast, buttons are big and easy to tap, and you can make the text even larger with the Text size button. On recipe pages, Cook mode keeps your screen from going dark while you cook, and you can check off ingredients as you add them.

Who we are

The Vintage Recipe Box is written and maintained by the Vintage Recipe Box team, home cooks who love old cookbooks, church supper tables, and the stories that come with a well-loved recipe. We test our recipes in ordinary home kitchens with ordinary equipment.

Advertising

To keep The Vintage Recipe Box free for everyone, we display advertisements from Google and other partners. Advertisers do not influence our recipes or articles. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
