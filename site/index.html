Welcome to The Vintage Recipe Box

The Vintage Recipe Box is a home for the recipes that used to live on stained index cards and in spiral-bound church cookbooks: Swedish meatballs from 1960s dinner parties, deviled eggs from every church supper, Depression-era wacky cake, and pies that won county fairs.

Every recipe includes a little history of the dish, along with clear modern measurements, oven temperatures, and food safety guidance. Our pages are easy to use in the kitchen: tap Cook mode to keep your phone screen awake, check off ingredients as you go, and use Text size at the top of any page to make the print larger.
